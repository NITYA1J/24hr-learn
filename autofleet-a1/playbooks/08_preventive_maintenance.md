# Preventive maintenance scheduling
Use when: failures cluster in categories with predictable wear such as brakes, tires and sensors.
Actions: 1) Schedule preventive work in low-demand hours using utilisation by hour. 2) Group preventive tasks per vehicle in a single visit. 3) Use mileage and age thresholds per category. 4) Track the ratio of planned to unplanned orders. 5) Review thresholds monthly against failure rates.
Owner: Fleet maintenance planner. Review: monthly.
