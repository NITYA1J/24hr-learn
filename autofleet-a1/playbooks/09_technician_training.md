# Technician training and certification
Use when: second-attempt rates are high or one category has long cycle times without a parts cause.
Actions: 1) Pair junior technicians with senior ones on the affected category. 2) Run a certification refresher on the procedure. 3) Create a short checklist for the most error-prone step. 4) Track first-time-fix rate by technician and category. 5) Recognise sites with the best first-time-fix rate and share their method.
Owner: Training lead. Review: monthly.
