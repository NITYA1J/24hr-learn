# Dispatch and routing around downtime
Use when: a site is below target and others are above target.
Actions: 1) Rebalance vehicles so healthy sites absorb demand from sites in backlog. 2) Prioritise returning the highest-utilisation vehicles to service first. 3) Avoid routing vehicles with open calibration warnings into long shifts. 4) Share a daily readiness board across sites. 5) Re-check readiness gap after rebalancing.
Owner: Dispatch lead. Review: daily.
