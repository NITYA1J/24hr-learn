# Bay capacity and throughput
Use when: bays are full and orders wait for a free bay rather than for parts or labour.
Actions: 1) Measure bay utilisation and turnover time. 2) Move quick jobs out of bays to mobile units. 3) Schedule long jobs such as body damage into fixed bays at off-peak times. 4) Add a second shift on bays if labour allows. 5) Evaluate adding a bay only after labour and parts are ruled out.
Owner: Site service manager. Review: weekly.
