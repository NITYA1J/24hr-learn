# Backlog older than 6 days
Use when: open work orders pile up in the 6+ day bucket or mean backlog age is high.
Actions: 1) Run a daily aging stand-up: every order older than 3 days needs an owner and an unblock date. 2) Separate parts-blocked orders from labour-blocked orders; they need different fixes. 3) Pull orders forward to mobile units when bays are the limit. 4) Escalate 6+ day orders to the regional lead. 5) Cap new low-severity intake until the 6+ bucket is cleared.
Owner: Site service manager. Review: daily.
